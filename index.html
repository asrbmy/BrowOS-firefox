Security

Lock screen

The lock screen covers your desktop. A PIN keeps other people from using it, but it is not strong security: anyone who can use your browser profile can still reach what BrowOS keeps in it. If you forget the PIN, open the browser's developer tools on this page and run localStorage.removeItem("browos-pin").

Sticky notes

0 notes

Drag widgets and notes by their title bar. Right-click the desktop to add a note quickly. The weather widget uses the city chosen in the Weather app.

Shortcuts

Click a key combination, then press the new keys. Backspace clears it and Esc cancels. Shortcuts work on the desktop and in the apps that come with BrowOS, not while a website app has the focus.

Data

Save your settings, wallpaper, notes, tasks and app data to a file, or bring them back from one. A backup is a plain file: anyone who has it can read it.
